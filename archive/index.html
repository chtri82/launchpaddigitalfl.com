Where Florida condo & HOA law stands after the 2026 session

What changed, what died, and the deadlines still ahead

The 2026 session was quiet for condo and HOA law: HB 657 died in committee, while HB 797 and HB 803 took effect July 1. The issue also recaps the SIRS, milestone-inspection and association-website deadlines still in force, how to attend and speak at board meetings, and the condo board election calendar.
