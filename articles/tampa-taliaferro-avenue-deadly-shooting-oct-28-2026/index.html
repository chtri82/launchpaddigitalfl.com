Man Shot and Killed on Taliaferro Avenue in Tampa; Sheriff's Office Says Detectives Believe It Was Isolated

Hillsborough County deputies found the man with upper-body trauma late Tuesday night. He died at a hospital, and the investigation is active.

By Maya Reyes · Published Oct. 28, 2026

AI-assisted journalist

Updated Oct 28, 2026

TAMPA, FL, Oct. 28, 2026 —

The Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office is investigating a deadly shooting in Tampa, according to the agency's news release issued at 2:53 a.m. Wednesday.

What the Sheriff's Office says happened

  • At 11:19 p.m. Tuesday, Oct. 27, deputies responded to the 12000 block of Taliaferro Avenue after receiving reports of shots being fired.
  • When deputies arrived, they found an adult male suffering from upper-body trauma.
  • He was taken to a local hospital, where he was pronounced dead.
  • "At this time, detectives believe this was an isolated incident," the release says.
  • "This remains an active investigation. Any updates will be provided as they become available."

The Sheriff's Office also shared the announcement in a post on its X account at 3 a.m. Wednesday.

What has not been released

As of early Wednesday, the Sheriff's Office hadn't released the man's name, information about a suspect or any arrest, or what led to the shooting. Launchpad Digital will add those details only when the Sheriff's Office releases them.

If you have information

The Sheriff's Office lists its non-emergency line as (813) 247-8200. Anonymous tips can go to Crime Stoppers at (800) 873-8477, the number the Sheriff's Office lists. In an emergency, call 911.

This is a developing story, and information from authorities is preliminary.

In response to Launchpad Digital's questions, Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office Public Relations Coordinator Hannah Oliveto said Wednesday, "As this is an active investigation, what we shared hours ago is all we have for release at this time." She said the agency forwarded a records request to its Records Division, which processes requests first-come, first-served.

Those are the facts, directly from the source.

Sources: Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office news release 26-178, Oct. 28, 2026; HCSO X post, Oct. 28, 2026; HCSO Public Relations Coordinator Hannah Oliveto, email to Launchpad Digital, Oct. 28, 2026.
