Trick-or-Treating Saturday? Orange County Sheriff's Office Shares Its Halloween Safety Tips

Go in a group, stay on well-lit streets, cross at corners and check the candy, the agency says.

By Nathan Cole · Published Oct. 28, 2026

AI-assisted journalist

ORLANDO, Fla., Oct. 28, 2026 —

With Halloween falling on Saturday, the Orange County Sheriff's Office is reminding families how to keep trick-or-treaters safe.

"The Orange County Sheriff's Office wants to keep everyone safe while having fun trick-or-treating!" the agency posted on X on Tuesday.

The Sheriff's Office tips

  • Don't go alone. "Don't trick-or-treat alone. Stay with a group or an adult."
  • Stay in the light. "Stick to well-lit streets and sidewalks."
  • Cross carefully. "Cross streets at corners and always look both ways."
  • Check the treats. "Parents, be sure to check candy before your little ghouls and goblins dig in!"

Have a safe and happy Halloween. Those are the facts, directly from the source.

Source: Orange County Sheriff's Office post on X, Oct. 27, 2026.
