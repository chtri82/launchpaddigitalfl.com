Alachua County Puts Road Repairs in the Fast Lane

County commissioners approved a plan to speed up funding for road projects, while crews report thousands of potholes filled. Here's what happened and what's next on the agenda.

By Claire Whitaker · Published Oct. 25, 2026

AI-assisted journalist

GAINESVILLE, Fla., Oct. 25, 2026 — Bumpy roads were front and center at the Alachua County Commission's Oct. 13 meeting. Commissioners approved "an accelerated funding plan for projects in the Transportation Capital Improvement Plan," according to the county's official meeting highlights (Alachua County).

The Transportation Capital Improvement Plan is the county's list of planned road projects. Speeding up the funding is meant to move those projects forward sooner. The county posted video of the full discussion with its meeting highlights (Alachua County).

Thousands of potholes patched

Before the vote, Communications Director Mark Sexton updated commissioners on the county's pothole work. Since the expanded pothole program launched last month, Public Works has repaired 4,662 potholes using 178.64 tons of materials, the county said (Alachua County).

Crews have also strip-milled and resurfaced 5,510 linear feet of roadway, using 776.95 tons of materials. Outside contractors have resurfaced another 7,539.88 linear feet, using 685.67 tons of materials (Alachua County).

Residents who spot a pothole or another roadway problem can report it through the county's Pothole Portal (Alachua County).

Other business on Oct. 13

The commission also approved the second reading of a measure to revise its firearms ordinance "to ensure compliance with state law" (Alachua County).

Commissioners heard a short history reading from the Alachua County Historical Commission about the 1923-24 First Baptist Church. They also heard an annual presentation from the Victim Services Rape Crisis Center Advisory Council (Alachua County).

There was some good news for nature fans too. Sexton shared a video on a new wildlife observation platform at Turkey Creek Preserve, where a trail was dedicated to the late Bob Simons, a longtime local conservationist (Alachua County).

Proclamations named Oct. 21 Lights on Afterschool Day and Oct. 22 Central Florida Community Action Day, and October American Pharmacists Month (Alachua County).

What's next: Oct. 27

The commission's next regular meeting is Tuesday, Oct. 27, starting at 11:30 a.m. in the Jack Durrance Auditorium of the County Administration Building, 12 SE 1st St., Gainesville. The meeting will reconvene at 5 p.m. (Alachua County).

Daytime items include a quarterly update from the Department of the Jail, a performance evaluation of administrative officials and a preliminary development plan for Westside Christian School (Alachua County).

In the evening, the board will hold public hearings on a comprehensive plan amendment and a planned development rezoning for the Everlee Farmstead Community (Alachua County).

How to take part

People can comment in person or call in during the noon comment period at 1-929-205-6099, meeting ID 873 5974 1977. Callers get three minutes on items not on the agenda and three minutes on agenda items (Alachua County).

The meeting airs on Cox Channel 12, the AC TV app and the county's Facebook and YouTube pages (Alachua County).
