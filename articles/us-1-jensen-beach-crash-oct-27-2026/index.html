Crash Shuts Southbound U.S. 1 Near Treasure Coast Square Mall for Hours Tuesday; Lanes Now Open

The Florida Highway Patrol is investigating an early-morning crash with injuries at NW Federal Highway and NW Jensen Beach Boulevard in Martin County.

By Maya Reyes · Published Oct. 27, 2026

AI-assisted journalist

JENSEN BEACH, FL, Oct. 27, 2026 —

Updated Oct. 27, 2026, 6:24 p.m. ET: Adds the Stuart Police Department's account of a pursuit and wrong-way crash, and the drivers' conditions.

A crash early Tuesday closed every southbound lane of U.S. 1 near the Treasure Coast Square Mall in Martin County during the morning commute. All southbound lanes have since reopened.

Troopers said they were investigating a crash with injuries at NW Federal Highway and NW Jensen Beach Boulevard, CBS12 reported. The Florida Highway Patrol is leading the investigation, and the Martin County Sheriff's Office and Stuart Police were also on scene, WPTV reported. WPBF also reported the closure and FHP's investigation.

Update: police describe a pursuit and wrong-way crash

The Stuart Police Department says the crash followed a police pursuit, according to WPTV and CBS12, which both reported the department's account:

  • At about 3:45 a.m., officers doing traffic enforcement near Southwest Federal Highway and Southwest St. Lucie Crescent saw a northbound vehicle traveling at what they described as an extremely high rate of speed. Police said the driver didn't stop when an officer tried to pull the vehicle over.
  • Police said the pursuit continued onto U.S. 1, where the driver began traveling north in the southbound lanes from Wright Boulevard, and officers tried to alert other drivers.
  • The vehicle collided with a southbound vehicle at the Mall South Entrance intersection, police said. Officers learned afterward that the driver of the fleeing vehicle was 15 years old.
  • Both drivers were taken to Lawnwood Medical Center in Fort Pierce with serious injuries.
  • The Stuart Police Department is investigating, and the Florida Highway Patrol is conducting a separate investigation into the crash. Police said no more information would be released while the investigations continue.

Launchpad Digital doesn't name minors.

What drivers should know

  • The Martin County Sheriff's Office posted on Facebook that the southbound lanes reopened at around 7:58 a.m., CBS12 reported. WPTV reported all southbound lanes were open by 7:15 a.m.
  • The crash also slowed northbound traffic, and drivers had been told to use other routes.

This is a developing story. Information from authorities is preliminary and may change, and we will update it when FHP or Stuart police release more details.

Those are the facts, directly from the source.

Sources: CBS12 (WPEC), WPTV and WPBF reporting on the Florida Highway Patrol, Martin County Sheriff's Office and Stuart Police Department response, Oct. 27, 2026.
