More Than 3.1 Million Floridians Have Already Voted in the Nov. 3 General Election

State figures compiled Tuesday morning show about 1.66 million early votes and 1.49 million mail ballots returned, with nearly 1 million mail ballots still out. Here is the count by party and what voters still need to know.

By Maya Reyes · Published Oct. 27, 2026

AI-assisted journalist

TALLAHASSEE, Fla., Oct. 27, 2026 —

More than 3.15 million Florida voters had cast ballots in the Nov. 3, 2026, General Election as of the Florida Division of Elections' statewide statistics compiled at 8:07 a.m. Tuesday, Oct. 27. The state builds these totals from daily reports filed by all 67 county Supervisors of Elections, and they run through the prior day's activity.

The statewide numbers

Voted early 727,255 601,289 40,895 290,959 1,660,398

The totals are counts of voters who have cast ballots, sorted by the party each voter is registered with. They are not results. No votes are counted publicly until polls close on Election Day, and a voter's party registration does not show how that person voted.

The largest counties so far

By the state's figures, the counties with the most early votes are Miami-Dade (162,101), Broward (143,471), Palm Beach (104,949), Orange (104,353) and Hillsborough (99,926). The counties with the most returned mail ballots are Miami-Dade (138,828), Pinellas (129,099), Palm Beach (117,654), Hillsborough (100,092) and Broward (99,739).

What voters still need to know

  • Mail ballots must arrive by 7 p.m. on Nov. 3. Under section 101.67, Florida Statutes, a vote-by-mail ballot counts only if the Supervisor of Elections receives it by 7 p.m. on Election Day. A postmark is not enough. Ballot drop-off options and hours are set by each county Supervisor of Elections.
  • Early voting dates and sites vary by county. Each county's dates, hours and locations are posted by its Supervisor of Elections.
  • Check your status. Voters can confirm their registration, polling place and mail ballot status through the Division of Elections' voter lookup.
  • Election Day. Under section 100.011, polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. local time on Tuesday, Nov. 3. Florida law, section 101.043, requires voters at the polls to show identification with a photo and a signature.

Launchpad Digital will report the updated state figures each day until results are certified.

Those are the facts, directly from the source.

Sources: Florida Division of Elections, Vote-by-Mail Request & Early Voting Statistics, 2026 General Election (compiled Oct. 27, 2026, 8:07 a.m.); section 101.67, Florida Statutes (Florida Senate); Florida Department of State.
