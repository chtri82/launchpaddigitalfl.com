Trump Holds a Midterm Rally in Daytona Beach Tonight; Expect Road Closures Around the Ocean Center

Doors open at 3:30 p.m. and the president is expected to speak at 7 p.m. Republican governor nominee Byron Donalds is expected to join him, four days before the Nov. 3 election.

By Ryan Castillo · Published Oct. 30, 2026

AI-assisted journalist

Updated Oct. 31, 2026, 8:15 a.m. ET: The rally took place Friday night; what Trump said.

DAYTONA BEACH, FL, Oct. 30, 2026 — President Donald Trump is scheduled to hold a midterm rally Friday night, Oct. 30, at the Ocean Center, 101 N. Atlantic Ave., according to the Republican Party's event page. The page lists the start time as 7 p.m., with doors opening at 3:30 p.m.

Byron Donalds, the Republican nominee for governor, is expected to join Trump at the event, Spectrum News 13 reported. Spectrum News 13 also reported that Democratic nominee David Jolly, Donalds' opponent, planned a stop in Volusia County on Thursday, Oct. 29. Both tickets are on Launchpad's list of statewide candidates: Donalds is running with Bryan Avila and Jolly with Gwen Graham.

Update, Oct. 31: What Trump said

Trump spoke for about 80 minutes at the Ocean Center on Friday night, according to the Roll Call Factba.se transcript. Republican nominees Byron Donalds (governor) and U.S. Sen. Ashley Moody also spoke.

"Four days from now, the people of Florida are going to elect Byron Donalds as your next governor," Trump said, according to the transcript. "And we're going to elect Ashley Moody ... to the U.S. Senate."

Donalds faces Democrat David Jolly, and Moody faces Democrat Angie Nixon, on Tuesday. Launchpad's list of statewide candidates has every name on the ballot.

Traffic and closures

The Daytona Beach Police Department told residents and visitors to expect significant traffic Friday, both Spectrum News 13 and FOX 35 Orlando reported. Both outlets list these closures:

  • Atlantic Avenue between Oak Ridge Boulevard and Main Street, 5 p.m. to 9 p.m.
  • Peninsula Drive between Main Street and Ora Street, starting at 4 p.m.

Police said more temporary closures will happen along the president's route, which won't be announced in advance, Spectrum News 13 reported. FOX 35 reported that travelers using Daytona Beach International Airport should allow extra time.

Tickets are free, Spectrum News 13 reported, and the event page allows up to four per signup. Everyone goes through security screening, and bags can't be larger than 18 by 13 by 7 inches, FOX 35 reported.

Voting deadlines

Under section 101.657, Florida Statutes, early voting must run through the third day before the election, which is Saturday, Oct. 31. Supervisors of elections may also open early voting on the second day before, Sunday, Nov. 1, so check your county's schedule. Polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Election Day, Tuesday, Nov. 3.

Launchpad will report on what the president says once his remarks are on the record.

If you're headed downtown tonight, give yourself extra time.

Sources: GOP event page; Spectrum News 13 and FOX 35 Orlando, citing the Daytona Beach Police Department; Florida Statutes.
