More Than 3.5 Million Floridians Have Now Voted in the Nov. 3 General Election

Nearly 370,000 more ballots were cast in a single day, the state's figures show. Early votes now top 1.9 million, and about 847,000 mail ballots are still out six days before Election Day.

By Nathan Cole · Published Oct. 28, 2026

AI-assisted journalist

TALLAHASSEE, FL, Oct. 28, 2026 —

More than 3.52 million Florida voters had cast ballots in the Nov. 3 General Election as of the Florida Division of Elections' statewide statistics compiled at 8:07 a.m. Wednesday, Oct. 28. That's 367,623 more than the 3,152,685 the state reported a day earlier.

The state builds these totals from daily reports filed by all 67 county Supervisors of Elections, and they run through the prior day's voting.

The statewide numbers

These are counts of voters who have cast ballots, sorted by each voter's party registration. They aren't results. No votes are tallied publicly until polls close on Election Day, and a voter's registration doesn't show how that person voted.

Where the most ballots have come in

By the state's county figures, the counties with the most early votes so far are Miami-Dade (185,333), Broward (165,098), Palm Beach (121,230), Orange (120,542) and Hillsborough (115,811). The most returned mail ballots are in Miami-Dade (147,509), Pinellas (137,488), Palm Beach (124,435), Hillsborough (106,089) and Broward (105,823).

What voters still need to know

  • Mail ballots must arrive by 7 p.m. on Nov. 3. Under section 101.67, Florida Statutes, a vote-by-mail ballot counts only if the county Supervisor of Elections receives it by 7 p.m. on Election Day. A postmark isn't enough.
  • Early voting dates, hours and sites vary by county. Each county's schedule is posted by its Supervisor of Elections.
  • Check your status. Voters can confirm their registration, polling place and mail ballot status with the Division of Elections' voter lookup.
  • Election Day. Under section 100.011, polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. local time on Tuesday, Nov. 3, and section 101.043 requires voters at the polls to show identification with a photo and a signature.

Launchpad Digital will report the state's updated figures each day until results are certified.

Those are the numbers, straight from the state. What they mean is yours to weigh.

Sources: Florida Department of State, Division of Elections, vote-by-mail and early voting statistics compiled Oct. 27 and Oct. 28, 2026; Florida Statutes.
