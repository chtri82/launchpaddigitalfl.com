About us

A facts-first Florida media company.

Launchpad Digital L.L.C. is based in Palm Bay, Florida. Our motto is simple: Where Florida gets clear answers.

Our mission

Launchpad Digital is a Florida media company. In the words of our editor, Charlie Triantafilou, our goal is “to bring Floridians the facts and let them make informed decisions instead of forcing our opinions on our readers, the people who keep the lights on.”

Floridians face a steady stream of news, new laws and public decisions that affect their homes, their money and their communities. We report what happened, explain it in plain language and point you to the original source so you can check it yourself. The goal is simple: give you the knowledge to make informed decisions for yourself, your family and your home.

Our journalists are AI-assisted.

What we publish

  • Launchpad Digital newsletter: our main publication. Original, sourced reporting on Local, State and National News, plus Money, Weather, Sports, Crime, Entertainment, Style & Fashion, Humanity, Education & DIY and Seasonal. Money covers budgeting and saving for Florida families, building wealth and generational wealth, and investing. Subscribe free.
  • Florida Condo & HOA Brief: a free weekly newsletter on condo and HOA laws and deadlines.
  • Getaways: practical Florida travel guides built around official park information.

We are not financial advisors; this is general information, not financial advice.

What we believe

  • Floridians deserve facts, not spin: clear, sourced information they can use to make informed decisions.
  • Readers deserve plain language, not jargon.
  • We don't tell readers what to think. We stay nonpartisan and make no endorsements.
  • Every claim should be traceable to a source.
  • We answer to readers. Sponsors never influence coverage, and sponsored content is always labeled.
  • When we get something wrong, we fix it in the open.

Who we are

Launchpad Digital L.L.C. is a Florida limited liability company in Palm Bay. Our editor is Charlie Triantafilou.
