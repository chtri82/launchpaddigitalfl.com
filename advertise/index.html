Our advertising rules

  • Sponsored content is always labeled. Ads, sponsored articles and featured listings are marked Sponsored and kept visually separate from our reporting and editorial picks.
  • No influence on coverage. Advertisers don't see, review or shape our stories. Buying an ad doesn't buy coverage, a recommendation or silence.
  • We correct mistakes openly. See our corrections policy.
  • No legal advice. Our content, sponsored or not, is general information, not legal, tax or financial advice.
  • Our right to decline. We may decline or remove any ad that conflicts with our editorial standards.

Accepted with labels and legal compliance

  • Political candidate, party and issue ads, clearly labeled as paid political advertising with the sponsor named, as Florida law requires. Political ad space is offered to all candidates and sides at the same rates and terms.
  • Firearms, ammunition and Second Amendment advertisers, following federal and Florida law
  • Licensed cannabis, tobacco and vaping businesses, following Florida advertising rules and age limits
  • Licensed gambling and alcohol advertisers

What we don't accept

  • Payday loans, title loans and other predatory lending
  • Get-rich-quick, MLM and crypto investment schemes
  • Unproven health or weight-loss claims
  • Adult content, and misleading or deceptive ads
