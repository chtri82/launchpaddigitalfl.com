Our sponsorship rules

Sponsors help keep the Brief free for readers. To protect readers' trust, every sponsorship follows these rules:

  • Always labeled. Sponsored messages are clearly marked as sponsored and kept visually separate from our reporting.
  • No influence on coverage. Sponsors don't see, review or shape our stories, and sponsoring doesn't buy coverage or keep a topic out.
  • Florida businesses only. We accept sponsors that operate in Florida.
  • Our right to decline. We may decline any sponsorship, including political or issue advocacy, or anything that conflicts with our editorial standards.

Common questions

How do I book a sponsorship?

Email editor@condohoabrief.com with your business name, location and what you'd like to share. We'll reply with available issues.

Can I sponsor if my business is outside Florida?

Not right now. Sponsorships are open to Florida businesses only.

Will you write about my business?

No. Sponsorship is separate from coverage. Sponsoring doesn't lead to stories, reviews or endorsements.

Do you accept political ads?

No. We're nonpartisan and don't accept political or issue-advocacy sponsorships.
