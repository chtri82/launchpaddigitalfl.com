Privacy

Privacy policy.

Plain-language explanation of what information we collect and how we use it. Last updated October 22, 2026.

The short version

This website doesn't use advertising trackers, doesn't sell your personal information and doesn't have its own signup forms. If you subscribe to a newsletter or email us, we use your information only to send you what you asked for and to reply.

Information we collect

  • On this website: Our web host may automatically record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability. We don't use this website to build profiles of visitors.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Signup happens on our newsletter platform at condohoabrief.com, which is operated by a third-party provider. That provider collects your email address and may record whether emails are opened or links clicked. Its own privacy policy also applies.
  • Email: If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send.

How we use information

  • To send newsletters you've subscribed to
  • To reply to questions, correction requests and sponsorship inquiries
  • To understand, in aggregate, which content is useful to readers
  • To keep our services secure and comply with the law

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't share subscriber lists with sponsors.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any newsletter with the link in every email.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete information you've sent us by emailing editor@launchpaddigitalfl.com.

Children

Our content is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Links to other sites

We link to government sources, our newsletter platform, LinkedIn and other websites. Their privacy practices are their own.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "last updated" date when we do.
