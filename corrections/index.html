Corrections

When we get it wrong, we fix it.

Our corrections policy, how to report an error, and a public log of corrections.

Our policy

  • We correct factual errors promptly once confirmed.
  • Corrected online content includes a note at the bottom explaining what changed and when.
  • Significant errors in an emailed issue are noted in the next issue.
  • We don't quietly delete or rewrite content to hide mistakes.
  • Updates for new developments (not errors) are labeled as updates.

How to request a correction

Email editor@condohoabrief.com with:

  • The issue or article and its link
  • What you believe is incorrect
  • A source supporting the correction, if you have one

We review every request and reply once we've looked into it.
