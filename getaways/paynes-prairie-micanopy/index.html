Paynes Prairie: wild bison and horses south of Gainesville

Florida's first state preserve, next to the small town of Micanopy, is the only place in the state where visitors can see wild-roaming bison and horses.

By Grant Holloway
AI-assisted journalist

As of October 2026, check hours, fees and closures before you go.

At a glance

Length
Day trip
Region
North Central Florida
Reviews
Yelp: 4.6 stars from 64 reviews (checked October 28, 2026).

Why go

Florida's first state preserve, next to the small town of Micanopy, is the only place in the state where visitors can see wild-roaming bison and horses. Reviewers have rated it well: see the review line above.

Itinerary

Day trip

  1. Morning

    Enter at 100 Savannah Blvd. in Micanopy and climb the park's 50-foot observation tower for views across the prairie.

  2. Midday

    Walk one of the park's eight trails and watch for bison, horses, alligators and some of the nearly 300 bird species the park lists.

  3. Afternoon

    Bike part of the 16-mile paved Gainesville-Hawthorne State Trail, or fish and canoe on Lake Wauburg.

Park, hours & fees

  • Paynes Prairie Preserve State Park (Florida State Parks)
    100 Savannah Blvd., Micanopy. Open 8 a.m. to sunset, 365 days a year. $6 per vehicle.

Hours, fees and address as listed on the official page when we checked on October 28, 2026.

Practical tips

  • Wild bison and horses are wild animals; keep your distance.
  • Bring binoculars for the observation tower.
  • Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.
