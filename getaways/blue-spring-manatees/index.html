Central Florida springs day: Blue Spring and De Leon Springs

Two Volusia County state parks on the St. Johns River system, roughly an hour and three-quarters from Palm Bay.

As of October 2026, check hours, fees and closures before you go.

At a glance

From Palm Bay
About 1¾ hours northwest (estimate, no traffic)
Length
Day trip
Region
Central Florida

Itinerary

Day trip

  1. Morning

    Arrive at Blue Spring State Park in Orange City early; the park often reaches capacity. In winter, manatees gather in the spring run to stay warm.

  2. Midday

    Drive roughly 30 to 40 minutes north to De Leon Springs State Park. The Old Spanish Sugar Mill restaurant is in the park.

  3. Afternoon

    Swim (in season), paddle or walk the trails, or stop at a riverfront restaurant in DeBary on the way back south.

  4. Evening

    Home in under two hours.

Parks & fees

  • Blue Spring State Park (Florida State Parks)
    Open 8 a.m. to sunset. $6 per vehicle. Frequently reaches capacity and may close temporarily. Swimming season is April 1 to November 14; winter is manatee season.
  • De Leon Springs State Park (Florida State Parks)
    Check the official page for fees and hours; we could not verify them for this guide.

Fees and hours as listed on each official page when we checked in October 2026.

Where to eat

Popular, long-running local picks. No paid placements.

  • Old Spanish Sugar Mill · De Leon Springs
    Restaurant at De Leon Springs; its site calls it a Central Florida staple since 1961. Source: official site.
  • Swamp House Riverfront Grill · DeBary
    Seafood and Florida dishes on the St. Johns River, according to its site. Source: official site.

Practical tips

  • For manatees, go in the cooler months; for swimming, go April 1 to November 14.
  • Weekend and holiday crowds can close the gates early. Arrive at opening.
  • Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.
