Estimates start from Orlando until you enter your own city or ZIP; your starting point is remembered on this device.
                                                                                  • Admission uses each attraction's published standard adult price × adults (2 if left blank), plus the published child/youth rate × children (age ranges vary; check the linked page), linked on its card, checked October 25, 2026. Children often pay less or get in free, so if you're bringing kids the real total is usually lower; check the linked price page. Park entry fees per vehicle assume one car.

                                                                                  • Fuel uses AAA's Florida average for regular gasoline, $4.11 per gallon on October 25, 2026, and an assumed 25 miles per gallon for one car, times round-trip road miles.

Drive time and road miles come from the free OSRM routing service without traffic; if it's unavailable we use straight-line miles × 1.25 at 50 mph.
