Getaways

Florida day trips and weekends from the Space Coast.

Six practical guides with itineraries, official park links and fees, and well-known places to eat. Each starts from Palm Bay.

As of October 2026, check hours, fees and closures before you go.

Space Coast · Day trip

A Space Coast day: wildlife drive, wild beach and launch country

Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge and Canaveral National Seashore sit right next to Kennedy Space Center, about an hour from Palm Bay.

From Palm Bay: about 1 hour north

Northeast Florida · 2 days

Two days in St. Augustine: the Castillo, the old city and Anastasia Island

The nation's oldest continuously occupied European-founded city is a straight run up I-95 from Brevard.

From Palm Bay: about 2½ hours north

South Florida · Long day trip

Everglades day trip: Shark Valley and the Tamiami Trail

Shark Valley, on the Tamiami Trail west of Miami, is the easiest way into the Everglades' sawgrass "river of grass."

From Palm Bay: about 3½ hours south

Central Florida · Day trip

Central Florida springs day: Blue Spring and De Leon Springs

Two Volusia County state parks on the St. Johns River system, roughly an hour and three-quarters from Palm Bay.

From Palm Bay: about 1¾ hours northwest

Florida Keys · 2 days

Two days in the Keys: Islamorada, Marathon and Bahia Honda

A Keys weekend from Brevard is mostly about the drive down the Overseas Highway. This plan stops in the Middle Keys rather than pushing to Key West.

From Palm Bay: about 4½ hours to islamorada

Tampa Bay · 2 days

Two days on Tampa Bay: Ybor City and the Gulf beaches

Historic Ybor City on day one, a Gulf barrier island on day two.

From Palm Bay: about 2½ hours west

Getaways guides are informational. We don't accept payment for listings, there are no sponsored or affiliate links, and we don't rate or review places. Restaurants are well-known, long-running local spots, each linked to its official site. Drive times are rough estimates from Palm Bay without traffic.

Why no Panhandle guide yet? Hurricane Isaias made landfall near Destin on October 9, 2026 (National Hurricane Center). We'll add one once park and road conditions there are clearer.
