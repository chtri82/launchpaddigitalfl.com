Myakka River State Park: Florida's first wild and scenic river

Fifty-eight square miles of wetlands, prairie and pinelands east of Sarasota, along the state's first designated wild and scenic river.

By Maya Reyes
AI-assisted journalist

As of October 2026, check hours, fees and closures before you go.

At a glance

Length
Day trip
Region
Southwest Florida

Why go

Fifty-eight square miles of wetlands, prairie and pinelands east of Sarasota, along the state's first designated wild and scenic river.

Itinerary

Day trip

  1. Morning

    Enter on State Road 72 east of Sarasota and stop at the Birdwalk, which the park recommends for viewing birds and alligators.

  2. Midday

    Lunch at the Myakka Outpost café inside the park, which also rents bikes and canoes.

  3. Afternoon

    Paddle, fish or bike the park's trails and backroads.

Park, hours & fees

  • Myakka River State Park (Florida State Parks)
    13207 State Road 72, Sarasota. Open 8 a.m. to sunset, 365 days a year. $6 per vehicle. The North Gate opens only on weekends and state holidays, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Drinking water is temporarily unavailable; bring your own (the ranger station offers free bottled water 8 a.m. to sunset).

Hours, fees and address as listed on the official page when we checked on October 28, 2026.

Food

The Myakka Outpost, inside the park, has a café, souvenirs and rentals, according to the park's page.

Local spots

We are still verifying local businesses for this guide and will add them once their ratings are confirmed.

Practical tips

  • Bring drinking water; park tap water is temporarily unavailable.
  • Alligators are common along the river; keep your distance.
  • Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.
