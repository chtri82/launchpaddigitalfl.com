Apalachicola: two small state museums in an old Gulf cotton port

A Franklin County fishing town on the Forgotten Coast, with an 1838 cotton merchant's home and a museum to the doctor who patented mechanical refrigeration.

By Ryan Castillo
AI-assisted journalist

As of October 2026, check hours, fees and closures before you go.

At a glance

Length
Day trip
Region
Panhandle

Why go

A Franklin County fishing town on the Forgotten Coast, with an 1838 cotton merchant's home and a museum to the doctor who patented mechanical refrigeration.

Itinerary

Day trip

  1. Morning

    Start at John Gorrie Museum State Park, 46 Sixth St. Dr. John Gorrie moved to Apalachicola in the early 1830s and in 1851 received the first U.S. patent for mechanical refrigeration; the museum displays a replica of his ice-making machine.

  2. Midday

    Walk to Orman House Historic State Park, 177 Fifth St., an 1838 home built by cotton merchant Thomas Orman overlooking the Apalachicola River. The park says the house mixes Federal and Greek Revival details. Its Veterans Memorial Plaza has a bronze replica of the Three Soldiers statue.

  3. Afternoon

    Eat downtown (see Local spots below) and walk the historic district near the river.

Park, hours & fees

  • John Gorrie Museum State Park (Florida State Parks)
    46 Sixth St., Apalachicola. Open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., Thursday through Monday, except Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day; closed Tuesday and Wednesday. $2 per person; children 5 and younger free.
  • Orman House Historic State Park (Florida State Parks)
    177 Fifth St., Apalachicola. Open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., Thursday through Monday; closed Tuesday and Wednesday. $2 per person museum admission. The Chapman Botanical Gardens have been closed for a construction project since Jan. 14, 2026; the park says all other amenities are open.

Hours, fees and address as listed on the official page when we checked on October 28, 2026.

Food

Both museums are in town, so downtown restaurants are a short walk or drive; see Local spots below.

Local spots

  • The Station Raw Bar (Apalachicola)
    A raw bar serving Apalachicola oysters, seafood and sandwiches at 53 Market St. Open Tuesday through Saturday, 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.; closed Sunday and Monday, according to its website.
    Yelp: 4.3 stars from 296 Yelp reviews (checked October 28, 2026; source listing).
  • Apalachicola Chocolate & Coffee Company (Apalachicola)
    A family-run shop in downtown Apalachicola that makes chocolates and caramels in its own kitchen and serves coffee, according to its website.
    Yelp: 4.4 stars from 157 Yelp reviews (checked October 28, 2026; source listing).

Practical tips

  • Both museums are closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays, so plan a Thursday-through-Monday visit.
  • The park lists Historic Christmas at Orman House in November and a historic tour of homes in May; check the park page for dates.
  • Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.
