Two days in St. Augustine: the Castillo, the old city and Anastasia Island

The nation's oldest continuously occupied European-founded city is a straight run up I-95 from Brevard.

As of October 2026, check hours, fees and closures before you go.

At a glance

From Palm Bay
About 2½ hours north (estimate, no traffic)
Length
2 days
Region
Northeast Florida

Itinerary

Day 1

  1. Morning

    Drive up I-95 and start at the Castillo de San Marcos National Monument, the 17th-century Spanish coquina fort on Matanzas Bay.

  2. Afternoon

    Walk the historic district around St. George Street and the Plaza de la Constitución. Most of it is free to stroll.

  3. Evening

    Dinner at the Columbia's St. Augustine location (see below).

Day 2

  1. Morning

    Cross the Bridge of Lions to Anastasia State Park for the beach, dunes and trails.

  2. Afternoon

    Lunch at O'Steen's, then head home, about 2½ hours.

Parks & fees

  • Castillo de San Marcos National Monument (National Park Service)
    $15 per adult 16 and older (valid 7 days); 15 and under free. Cashless.
  • Anastasia State Park (Florida State Parks)
    Open 8 a.m. to sunset. $8 per vehicle.

Fees and hours as listed on each official page when we checked in October 2026.

Where to eat

Popular, long-running local picks. No paid placements.

  • Columbia Restaurant, St. Augustine · Historic district
    Spanish and Cuban restaurant; the family-owned Columbia describes itself as dating to 1905. Source: official site.
  • O'Steen's Restaurant · St. Augustine
    Seafood and Southern dishes; its site says it opened in 1965 and is known for fried shrimp. Source: official site.

Practical tips

  • October through April is generally more comfortable for walking the old city than midsummer.
  • Downtown parking fills on weekends and event nights; plan for paid parking.
  • Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.
