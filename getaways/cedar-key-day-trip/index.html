Cedar Key: a small Gulf island town and its history museum

An island town on Florida's Nature Coast, with a state park museum, salt marshes and a historic waterfront.

By Nathan Cole
AI-assisted journalist

As of October 2026, check hours, fees and closures before you go.

At a glance

Length
Day trip or overnight
Region
Nature Coast
Reviews
Yelp (museum): 4.0 stars from 11 reviews (checked October 28, 2026).

Why go

An island town on Florida's Nature Coast, with a state park museum, salt marshes and a historic waterfront. Reviewers have rated it well: see the review line above.

Itinerary

Day trip

  1. Morning

    Start at Cedar Key Museum State Park, with exhibits on local history and St. Clair Whitman's collections of seashells and Native American artifacts. Naturalist John Muir spent a few months in Cedar Key in 1867; a state historic marker is on the grounds.

  2. Midday

    Take the short nature trail to the boat launch and look out over the salt marshes. Paddlers can put in a kayak or canoe there.

  3. Afternoon

    Walk the town's waterfront and Dock Street.

Park, hours & fees

  • Cedar Key Museum State Park (Florida State Parks)
    12231 S.W. 166th Court, Cedar Key. Open 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., Friday through Sunday. $2 per person; children 5 and younger free. The Whitman House has been temporarily closed since Dec. 20, 2024.

Hours, fees and address as listed on the official page when we checked on October 28, 2026.

Food

We have not listed restaurants for this guide. The town has a waterfront dining area; check current hours locally.

Local spots

Practical tips

  • The museum is open Friday through Sunday only, according to the park.
  • Cedar Key is at the end of State Road 24; plan fuel accordingly.
  • Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30.
