Morning Brief: Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026

New Launchpad stories since the Evening Brief come first, then earlier stories that are still developing.

By Launchpad Digital News Staff · Published Oct. 28, 2026, 7:45 AM ET

New since the last brief

  • Breaking · World Two High-Speed Trains Collide Near Urda, Spain; Rail Operator Renfe Says There Are Dead and Injured
    Spanish officials have activated a regional emergency plan in Toledo province. High-speed service between Madrid and Andalusia is suspended, and the train operators have opened hotlines for families.
  • State · Weather Coastal Flood Warnings Extended Into Friday on Much of Florida's Atlantic Coast; Keys Remain Under Major Flooding Warning
    The National Weather Service extended coastal flood warnings for Northeast and East Central Florida until 2 a.m. Friday, with rough surf and rip currents continuing; the Keys remain under a warning.
  • Breaking · Local Man Shot and Killed on Taliaferro Avenue in Tampa; Sheriff's Office Says Detectives Believe It Was Isolated
    Hillsborough County deputies found the man with upper-body trauma late Tuesday night. He died at a hospital, and the investigation is active.
  • State · Election Three Proposed Constitutional Amendments Are on Florida's Nov. 3 Ballot: What Each One Would Do, in the State's Own Words
    The ballot summaries for Amendments 1, 2 and 3, taken directly from the Florida Division of Elections, plus the 60% rule and key voting deadlines.
  • Local · Orange Trick-or-Treating Saturday? Orange County Sheriff's Office Shares Its Halloween Safety Tips
    Go in a group, stay on well-lit streets, cross at corners and check the candy, the agency says.
  • Local · Sarasota Sarasota County Residents Turn In More Than 46 Pounds of Unwanted Medication at Sheriff's Office Take Back Event
    The Sheriff's Office thanked the community for taking part in the DEA Drug Take Back event over the weekend.
  • Feel-Good · Jacksonville Jacksonville Sheriff's Office Shows How Its Vice Detectives and Animal Care Officers Rescue Neglected Dogs
    A new agency video follows emergency warrants in animal cruelty cases and the dogs taken to safety. Report suspected cruelty at 904-630-0500.

Earlier today

  • Breaking · National Trump Announces EPA Proposal to Revise Biden-Era Methane Rules for Oil and Gas Wells
    The EPA says the proposal would save $45 billion and add an estimated 210 million barrels of oil; it is a proposal, not a final rule, with at least 45 days for public comment.
  • Breaking · National First Venezuelan Oil Cargo Under U.S. Deal Arrives in Corpus Christi as Trump and Rubio Visit the Port
    The White House says the 1 million barrels will be refined in Texas; the producer says the first cargo came at no cost to taxpayers under an August agreement.
  • State · Election Who's Running Statewide in Florida on Nov. 3: The Qualified Candidates, From the State's Own List
    The state's list of qualified candidates for U.S. Senate, governor, attorney general, chief financial officer and agriculture commissioner, in the order the state lists them.
  • State · Election More Than 3.1 Million Floridians Have Already Voted in the Nov. 3 General Election
    State figures compiled Tuesday show about 1.66 million early votes and 1.49 million mail ballots returned, with nearly 1 million mail ballots still out.
  • National · Election Trump Rallies in Corinth, Mississippi, for Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith, Urges Early Voting
    Speaking eight days before the Nov. 3 midterm election, the president asked supporters to vote, according to the official video.
  • State (Panhandle) Hurricane Isaias Recovery Centers in the Panhandle Begin Closing; Santa Rosa Site's Last Day Is Wednesday
    The state's in-person recovery centers in Santa Rosa, Washington, Escambia and Walton counties close between Oct. 28 and Oct. 31; Okaloosa's has no closing date yet.
  • Local (Hillsborough) · Feel-good Hillsborough Sheriff's Office Celebrates Its 2026 Class of Volunteer Citizen Patrol Graduates
    Residents who trained in crime prevention and first aid will volunteer as an extra set of eyes and ears for the Sheriff's Office.
  • Breaking · World Localized Russia-Ukraine Ceasefire Takes Effect in Oleshky So Aid Can Reach Trapped Residents
    A truce the White House says First Lady Melania Trump brokered began early Tuesday in and around the Russian-occupied city so aid can enter and residents who want to leave can be evacuated; both sides said it was being observed.
  • National Federal Survey: Rate of Serious Violent Victimization Fell 25% in 2025, Preliminary Estimates Show
    Preliminary Bureau of Justice Statistics estimates show serious violent victimization down 25% and property victimization down 19%; the numbers may change in the final 2027 report.
  • Breaking · State DeSantis Order Names John Anderson's "Seminole Wind" Florida's Anthem
    Executive Order 26-222 creates a separate "Florida's Anthem" designation, directs markers in Apopka and a renamed Lake Apopka wildlife drive, and leaves the 2008 state anthem law in place.
  • Money U.S. Consumer Confidence Rises 7.5 Points in October as Gas Prices Ease, Conference Board Says
    The Conference Board's index rose to 91.7 from 84.2; its economist said confidence remains on a long-term downward trend.
  • Local (Hillsborough) · Good news Two Boaters Brought Safely to Shore After Sailboat Capsizes Near Tampa's Ballast Point Pier
    Sheriff's deputies righted a flipped 16-foot sailboat, brought both boaters aboard and towed the boat to shore.
