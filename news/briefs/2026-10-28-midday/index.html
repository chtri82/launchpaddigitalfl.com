Midday Brief: Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026

New Launchpad stories since the Morning Brief come first, then this morning's stories, with any verified updates marked.

By Launchpad Digital News Staff · Published Oct. 28, 2026, 1:35 PM ET

New since the last brief

  • State · Election More Than 3.5 Million Floridians Have Now Voted in the Nov. 3 General Election
    Nearly 370,000 more ballots were cast in a single day, the state's figures show. Early votes now top 1.9 million, and about 847,000 mail ballots are still out six days before Election Day.
  • Breaking · State Florida AG Charges Kissimmee Man With 100 Felony Counts of Child Sexual Abuse Material, Sues AI Platform A2E
    Attorney General James Uthmeier says the company behind A2E lets users make sexualized images of real Floridians, including children. He wants a court to stop it.
  • Breaking · State AG Uthmeier Says His Office Is Launching an Investigation Into a 'CCP-Controlled Corporation' Operating in Florida
    The attorney general's post quoted a video about yacht maker Ferretti Group and its Fort Lauderdale property. Here's what the official records show and what Florida law says.
  • Breaking · World U.S. Treasury Sanctions Rwanda's Ministry of Defence and a Rwandan Bank Over Support for M23 Rebels
    Treasury says Rwanda's defense ministry directed troops fighting alongside the M23 armed group in eastern Congo, and that the Zigama Credit and Savings Society moved money and gold for them.
  • World U.K. Announces £10 Billion in New Support for Ukraine, Its Largest Package Yet
    The British government says the money includes £500 million for Ukraine's air defenses this winter. It was announced alongside the U.K.'s 2026 Budget.
  • World U.S. Treasury Sanctions 10 People and a Russia-Based Center Over North Korean IT Worker Schemes
    Treasury says North Korean tech workers use fake identities to get hired by U.S. companies, and the regime keeps most of their pay to fund its weapons programs.
  • World Israel Counts Votes After Election; Partial Counts Show Neither Bloc at a 61-Seat Majority
    Partial, unofficial counts reported by news outlets put Netanyahu's bloc at 54 seats and Eisenkot's at 49, with soldiers' ballots still to be counted.
  • World India and Mexico Formally Launch Talks on a Preferential Trade Agreement
    The two governments signed terms of reference that set the scope and roadmap for negotiations, India's Commerce Ministry says.
  • World Five European Defense Ministers Pledge a Bigger Role for Europe Within NATO
    France, Germany, Italy, Poland and the U.K. said the U.S. "is and will remain an indispensable Ally" and reaffirmed support for Ukraine.
  • World Ebola Outbreak in Congo Nears 10,000 Confirmed Cases as Kenya and Nigeria Report Imported Cases
    European and WHO health officials say the risk to travelers remains low, and WHO advises against travel or trade restrictions.
  • World A Horse Rider's Sharp Eyes Help New Zealand Remove 141 Invasive Plants From Ninety Mile Beach
    New Zealand's Department of Conservation says one beachgoer's photos and phone call led crews to a hidden patch of sea spurge.
  • World India Sends Federal Team to Investigate a Cheetah's Death From Pellet Injuries
    The 19-month-old male was born in India to a cheetah brought in under Project Cheetah, the country's effort to reintroduce the big cats.
  • Sports Panthers Score Three Power-Play Goals in 5-1 Win at Winnipeg
    Carter Verhaeghe scored twice, Matthew Tkachuk had four assists and Brady Tkachuk added a goal and two assists. The two teams meet again Friday in Sunrise.
  • Getaways Apalachicola: two small state museums in an old Gulf cotton port
    A Franklin County fishing town on the Forgotten Coast, with an 1838 cotton merchant's home and a museum to the doctor who patented mechanical refrigeration.
  • Getaways Weeki Wachee Springs: the mermaid show and a first-magnitude spring
    A classic Old Florida attraction in Hernando County, where mermaids perform in water that flows from a first-magnitude spring.
  • Humanity · Feel-good Florida Agencies Thank the People Behind the Badge on National First Responders Day
    Police and sheriff's offices from Orlando to Tampa used Oct. 28 to recognize officers, deputies, dispatchers and the newest 911 call-takers.
