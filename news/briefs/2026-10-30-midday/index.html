Midday Brief: Friday, Oct. 30, 2026

Coastal flooding from Jacksonville to the Keys, 4.27 million Floridians have voted, and the rest of Friday's news, every fact linked to its source.

By Launchpad Digital News Staff · Published Oct. 30, 2026, 1:45 PM ET

New since the last brief

  • Local (Monroe) Weather Service Warns of 'Major Saltwater Flooding' in the Florida Keys This Evening
    A coastal flood warning covers the Upper, Middle and Lower Keys until 11 p.m. Friday. Numerous roads may close.
  • Local (Lake/Volusia) St. Johns River Floods Near Astor; Some Homes on Wildhog and Bonnet Roads Cut Off, Forecasters Say
    Moderate flooding is happening near Astor, and minor flooding is forecast near DeLand, the National Weather Service in Melbourne says.
  • State · Election More Than 4.27 Million Floridians Have Voted Ahead of Tuesday's Election, State Data Show
    About 2.5 million voted early in person and nearly 1.77 million returned mail ballots, according to the Division of Elections.
  • State 'Flying Cars' Fly Over Tampa Bay in Florida's First eVTOL Demonstration Flights, DeSantis Says
    Three types of electric air taxis retraced the route of the world's first commercial airline flight, part of an FAA pilot program.
  • State Coastal Flooding Continues From Jacksonville to the Keys; State Warns Drivers About Saltwater
    Warnings and advisories cover much of Florida's Atlantic coast, and rip currents stay dangerous through the weekend.
  • Nation DHS Says ICE Arrested 4,170 People in a 10-Day Texas Enforcement Operation
    The department says the 'Lone Star Mission' ran Oct. 19 to Oct. 29 with state and local partners.
  • Nation Syrian Hacker Who Worked for Assad's Regime Gets 42 Months; DOJ Says One Hack Briefly Erased $136 Billion in Stock Value
    Ahmad Umar Agha hacked about 47 Western media outlets, businesses, NGOs and government entities, the Justice Department says.
  • World EU Creates an Expert Group to Prepare Europe for Wildfires and Extreme Weather, Including a Possible Firefighting Fleet
    The 17-member PreparEU Advisory Group meets in early November and reports to Ursula von der Leyen by spring 2027.
  • World 69 Dead After Two High-Speed Trains Collide Near Urda, Spain
    UPDATED: Castilla-La Mancha's government says the toll stands at 69 dead, with 39 still hospitalized and 71 discharged. Updated Oct. 30, 1:45 PM ET.
  • Money Americans' Spending Rose 0.5% in September as Prices Climbed 3.6% From a Year Earlier, BEA Says
    Personal income rose 0.2%, and the saving rate was 3.8%. After inflation, disposable income fell 0.3%.
  • Money Alphabet Says Revenue Rose 25% to $127.6 Billion; Google Cloud Sales More Than Doubled
    The Google parent reported third-quarter earnings of $2.12 a share. Google Services revenue grew 11%.
  • Crime Six Sentenced in Tampa in a Smuggling and Asylum-Fraud Scheme Behind Over 1,700 Fake Claims, DOJ Says
    Sentences range from two to four years. Prosecutors say migrants paid $1,500 to $40,000 and some were flown in on chartered planes.
  • Crime Personal Assistant Gets 48 Months for Stealing Nearly $10 Million From Elderly Employers, Spending It at Louis Vuitton and Gucci
    Prosecutors say Catalina Corona cashed hundreds of checks and hid more than 300 luxury items in two storage units.
  • Science & Technology NASA Asks Companies How They Could Boost, Service or Safely Bring Down the Hubble Space Telescope
    Hubble is likely to re-enter the atmosphere around 2033 to 2034, so a mission would need to fly by 2032, NASA says. Responses are due Nov. 30.
  • Science & Technology Hurricane Center Releases Its Final Report on July's Tropical Storm Bertha, Which Brought Flooding to the Panhandle Coast
    The report calls Bertha 'a highly sheared tropical storm' that caused 'minor-to-moderate coastal flooding' on the northern Gulf Coast.

  • Local (Volusia) · Election Trump Holds a Midterm Rally in Daytona Beach Tonight; Expect Road Closures Around the Ocean Center
    Doors open at 3:30 p.m. and the president is expected to speak at 7 p.m. Republican governor nominee Byron Donalds is expected to join him, four days before the Nov. 3 election.

    The independent newspaper says it was evicted from its Moscow office and cut off from reader donations. "We have been killed," its statement begins.
