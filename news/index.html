About this page

Headlines from around Florida are not our reporting. Headlines, short excerpts and links come from each outlet's public RSS feed and are refreshed automatically about every 15 minutes. Click any headline to read the full story on the source's site. Story selection and wording belong to each source.

Politics mixes outlets from across the spectrum (Florida Phoenix, Florida Politics, The Capitolist and public radio from WUSF and Central Florida Public Media), sorted only by time. Crime and budgeting are picked by keywords (for example "arrest," "sheriff," "budget," "property tax" or "insurance rates"), so some stories may be miscategorized. Weather alerts and forecasts come directly from the National Weather Service.

Your location is used only in your browser to look up your county with OpenStreetMap Nominatim and the National Weather Service. We don't store it on our servers. Spot a problem? Tell us.
